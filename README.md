# Saturday League — Football

A complete, playable browser football game built with plain HTML, CSS, and Canvas—no installs, frameworks, or external assets required.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a modern desktop or mobile browser.

- **WASD** or **arrow keys** — move your selected player
- **Shift** — sprint (stamina gradually recovers)
- **Space** — shoot when you have the ball
- **E** — pass to a teammate
- **Q** — switch to the player nearest the ball
- **Esc** — pause or resume

Touch controls are shown on small screens. The match has a running clock, score, goals, AI opponents, and automatic kickoffs after goals. A 90-second match ends and resets for another round.

## Matchday features

- A striped, marked pitch with center circle, penalty areas, corner flags, goals, and netting
- Animated spectators, stadium lighting, ad boards, a scoreboard sign, team benches, and a players' tunnel
- Illustrated players with heads, kits, numbers, shadows, and animated running limbs
- Player movement, sprint stamina, ball possession, passing, shooting, and opposing-team AI
- Responsive layout and keyboard plus touch controls

## Inspiration and scope

This is an original, lightweight 2D game inspired by the broad appeal of mobile football games such as Dream League Soccer: quick matches, team-versus-team play, goals, responsive controls, and a lively stadium. It does not copy DLS code, branding, art, or proprietary assets. A small Canvas project cannot reproduce the full 3D presentation and extensive career, club-management, transfer, online multiplayer, commentary, and licensed-team systems of a commercial game; those are larger future features rather than assets to copy.
